## BIOS setup waits for the host to boot — unreleased

`PollingBiosSetup` now completes only once the host's Redfish `BootProgress` shows the OS booting after the BIOS reboot. Previously it could complete while the BMC was still applying the settings with extra power cycles, and the DPU-wait state then restarted the DPU in the middle of its boot. BMCs that report no standard `BootProgress` keep the previous behavior.

### New and changed configuration

| Setting | Change |
| --- | --- |
| `machine_state_controller.bios_apply_settle_timeout` | New. Longest wait for the host to boot after the BIOS reboot; defaults to `20m`, must be greater than `0s` and at most `60m`. After it, the step completes and logs a warning. |
| `machine_state_controller.dpu_first_restart_delay` | New. Delay before the first restart of a DPU that has not reported. Unset keeps 5 minutes; when set, must be greater than `0s` and at most `30m`. |
