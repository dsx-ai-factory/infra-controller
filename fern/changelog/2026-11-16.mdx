## Upcoming: RMS firmware polling uses GetJobStatus

Builds containing [PR #9265](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory/infra-controller/pull/9265)
poll firmware-object jobs through `GetJobStatus` instead of `GetFirmwareJobStatus`.
This applies to rack firmware maintenance, power-shelf, NVSwitch, and compute-tray
status polling, including passive firmware-job reads.

Trace `GetJobStatus` RPCs for these firmware reads and update RED filters from
`operation="get_firmware_job_status"` to `operation="get_job_status"`.

NICo does not fall back to `GetFirmwareJobStatus` when the generic status RPC
is unavailable. NVOS system-image jobs use `GetSwitchSystemImageJobStatus`.

NICo accepts exactly one response record matching the tracked job ID. Missing,
duplicate, mismatched, or unrecognized status evidence cannot establish job
completion. See [RMS firmware-job polling](../../docs/architecture/backends/rms.md#rms-apis-nico-calls)
for response validation and each caller's missing-job behavior.
